# Volunteer Operations

Language for coordinating IEEE Rwanda volunteer activities, organizational responsibility, and service hours.

## Language

**Activity lead**:
The volunteer who created an activity and is responsible for approving its volunteer hours.
_Avoid_: Event owner, submitter

**Active volunteer**:
A volunteer assigned to an activity as a participant.
_Avoid_: General volunteer, roster member

**Unit chair**:
A volunteer designated to manage a specific organization and its activities, including child organizations within that unit.
_Avoid_: Unit admin

**Hour submission**:
A volunteer’s claimed time for one activity, pending approval by that activity’s lead.
_Avoid_: Logged hours, approved hours

**Approved volunteer hours**:
Service time approved or directly recorded by an activity lead and included in totals and certificates.
_Avoid_: Pending hours

**Event publication decision**:
Gentille Uwera’s decision to publish or not publish a submitted activity on the IEEE Rwanda public calendar.
_Avoid_: Event approval

**Fully approved funding request**:
A funding request approved by the Chair, the Treasurer, and either the Vice Chair or Secretary.
_Avoid_: Admin-approved request, single approval
